# Google Ads Campaign Types Reference

| Type | Best For | Avoid When |
|---|---|---|
| Search | High-intent leads, direct sales | No search demand exists for the product |
| Shopping | E-commerce product listings | No product feed / Merchant Center account |
| Display | Retargeting, brand awareness | Tight budget — CPCs are low but conversion rates are often lower |
| Performance Max | Multi-channel reach with limited management time | You need granular control over placements |
| Video (YouTube) | Top-of-funnel awareness, product demos | Direct-response with small budgets |
| App | Mobile app installs / in-app actions | Non-app products |
| Smart | Beginners with small local budgets | Advanced advertisers who need control |
